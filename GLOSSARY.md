# Course Platform

An online course platform where learners buy courses, watch lessons and track their progress.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to give back a Purchase and get their money back. It is Requested until it is Approved, Declined, or Withdrawn by the learner.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The return of exactly what the learner paid for a Purchase, which happens when their Refund Request is Approved. It ends their Enrollment in the course, and the Purchase stays on record as Refunded.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase may be the subject of a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, not too much of the course watched or completed, no Refund Request already open, and not a claimed team seat.

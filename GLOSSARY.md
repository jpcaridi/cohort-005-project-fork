# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the people involved and the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
Someone who buys and takes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Student, Customer, User, Investor

**Instructor**:
Someone who creates and owns courses. An Instructor can see the Refund Requests on their own courses but cannot decide them.
_Avoid_: Teacher, Author, Creator

**Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team who runs the platform. Only an Admin can approve or decline a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Staff, Moderator, Team (for a single person)

**Team Owner**:
Someone who buys seats on a course for a team and hands them out. People who claim a seat are Learners.
_Avoid_: Team Admin, Team Manager, Licence Holder, Seat Buyer

### Courses

**Duration**:
How long a lesson's video runs. Some lessons have no Duration recorded.
_Avoid_: Length, Runtime

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once. An Admin can also raise one on a learner's behalf, which is approved as it is raised.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Overdue Refund Request**:
An Open Refund Request that has waited longer than the Admins aim to take to decide it.
_Avoid_: Late, Stale, Expired

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner loses access to the course straight away, whatever the amount and whether or not the money has been sent yet.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason given to the learner. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is the full amount they paid unless it is a Partial Refund. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Partial Refund**:
A Refund for less than the learner paid, with the Admin's reason for the difference.

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent, optionally with the Admin's reason. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the Admin can retry it until it becomes an Issued Refund.
